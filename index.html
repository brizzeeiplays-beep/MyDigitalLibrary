How your savings are calculated

Quick add a book

Scan the ISBN barcode on the back of your manga, or search by ISBN/title. Choose a matching edition before saving. You can always enter everything manually below.

For best results, point the rear camera at the long 13-digit barcode starting with 978 or 979 (not the small 5-digit price barcode). Hold the book still in bright, even light and move the phone back slightly until the lines are sharp.

Take a close, sharp photo of the ISBN barcode, or choose an existing photo. If scanning fails, type the 13 digits in the ISBN or Book Title box.
